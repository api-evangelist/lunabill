For years, Revenue Cycle leaders have largely measured success around getting a claim out cleanly, getting it paid, and preventing denials. The Transforming Episode Accountability Model (TEAM) pushes us to think beyond the individual claim and look at the full episode of care. That changes where Revenue Cycle responsibility ends.
