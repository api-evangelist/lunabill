# LunaBill

> LunaBill is AI revenue cycle automation for hospitals, health systems, and RCM firms. A coordinated team of AI agents works the entire accounts receivable (AR) queue end to end: checking payer portals, calling insurance companies, working denials, filing appeals, and resubmitting claims, so billing teams review outcomes instead of sitting on hold.

LunaBill is a product of LunaHealth Technologies, Inc. It is backed by Y Combinator, HIPAA compliant, and SOC 2 Type I (Type II in progress).

## What LunaBill does

- Automates claim status calls to payers: Luna places calls, waits on hold, navigates IVR phone trees, and gets a live answer with no staff time.
- Retrieves status from payer portals automatically (Availity, Waystar, and payer-specific portals).
- Works denials, files appeals, and resubmits claims.
- Runs inside the EHR you already use (Epic, Cerner, GoRev) and your existing clearinghouses (Waystar, Availity).
- Leaves a full audit trail on every claim that RCM firms can hand to clients.

## Who it is for

- Hospitals and health systems that need to work a growing AR backlog without growing headcount.
- Revenue cycle management (RCM) and BPO firms that want to scale client portfolios without scaling payroll.

## Pricing

- Performance based: 1.5% of net collections recovered, billed only after the money lands.
- Nothing upfront, no license fees, no implementation fees. If LunaBill recovers nothing, you owe nothing.
